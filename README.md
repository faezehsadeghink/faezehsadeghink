## About Me

I am a **Geospatial Researcher** and an **M.Sc. student in Remote Sensing at the University of Tehran**. My interests include Earth observation, satellite image processing, spatial data analysis, web geovisualization, and the application of machine learning to geospatial problems.

I enjoy transforming raw spatial and satellite data into reproducible analyses, interactive maps, 3D visualizations, and practical research workflows.
